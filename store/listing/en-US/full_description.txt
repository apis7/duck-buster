Duck Buster turns duck season into a friendly competition for your family and hunting buddies.

LOG A HUNT IN SECONDS
After the hunt, pick who was there and type a few letters. The app knows North American ducks, geese, coots and teal, plus the names hunters actually use: "greenhead", "GWT", "bluebill", "can". Your most-logged birds come up first. Mark drake or hen, and flag a band when you get one.

THE BRAGGING BOARD
See who's leading the season, hunter by hunter or the whole crew together. Race charts, bird counts, greenheads, bands and head-to-head rivalries settle the arguments at the truck.

COLLECT AND EARN
• Duck-dex: fill a card for every species your crew takes. Rare birds come up shiny.
• Band trophy case for every banded bird.
• Dozens of secret badges, revealed only when someone earns them.
• Titles that grow as you hunt.
• Season Awards Night at the end of the year, with serious awards and a few roasts, plus your Season Wrapped recap.

THE DUCK SHOOT
Every time the app opens, warm up with a 12-second pixel duck-shoot practice round: drag the gun to aim, release to fire, and go for the vital zone for triple points. After each hunt, the day's top hunter plays the real round, and those scores count on the board.

LEARN YOUR DUCKS
The Duck ID quiz shows a photo or plays a call and gives you seven choices. Good practice for telling a gadwall from a hen mallard before the birds are in.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account and no sign-up. Your hunting log is stored only on your phone. Moving to a new phone works screen to camera with QR codes, and you can save a backup file whenever you like.

FREE, WITH ADS
Duck Buster is free and supported by family-friendly ads. They appear only when you're done with something, like after saving a hunt, never in the middle of logging or during the game.

Duck Buster is for fun and record keeping. Always follow your state and federal hunting regulations.
