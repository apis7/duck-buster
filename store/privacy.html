Duck Buster

Privacy Policy · Published by Apsinthion · Effective November 21, 2026

The short version: your hunting log never leaves your phone. Duck Buster has no account and no server of ours. The only thing in the app that uses the internet is Google's ad service (AdMob), which shows the ads that keep the app free.

What the app stores, and where

  • Hunters you add — names, nicknames and colors.
  • Hunts — dates, who hunted, and the birds logged (species, count, drake or hen, banded).
  • Game and quiz scores, badges, awards and your settings.

All of it is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We (Apsinthion) never receive it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Duck Buster shows ads from Google AdMob on a few browse-only screens and full-screen after you save a hunt. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects and sends to Google: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions with ads, and crash and diagnostic information. Your hunting log is never shared with Google or anyone else.

  • Ads are limited to content rated for general and parental-guidance audiences, and sensitive categories (such as dating, gambling and sexual content) are blocked.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

App updates

When a new version is on Google Play, the app may offer to update. It asks the Play Store app on your phone; it does not contact any server of ours.

Permissions

  • Internet and advertising ID — used only by the ad SDK described above.
  • Camera — optional; used only to scan a transfer code when moving your data to a new phone, and only while that screen is open. Camera frames are decoded on the device and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Vibrate — for haptic feedback.

The app does not request location, contacts or microphone access.

Moving to a new phone, and backups

Phone-to-phone transfer works screen to camera: one phone shows looping QR codes, the other reads them. No network, server or account is involved. You can also save a backup file and share it wherever you choose through Android's share sheet; where it goes is up to you. If Android's own device backup is on, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup; we never have access to it.

Deleting your data

Delete hunters and hunts in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
